# Tools — the workbench

Buy these **before module 04**, and the soldering set before
[02.07](../02-motors-escs-props/02.07-static-thrust-test.md) if you want to build the thrust rig.

Prices read 2026-09-22 or carried over from the robotics course's 2026-09-16 pass; ₪ including
VAT. If you did the robotics course you already own most of this list.

## Buy now

| Item | Spec that matters | ≈ ₪ | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soldering iron | **80 W or a temperature-controlled station.** A 30 W iron cannot solder 12 AWG to an XT60 — it will make a cold joint that fails at 40 A | 150–350 | Hackstore, KSP |
| Solder | 60/40 leaded, 0.8 mm, rosin core. Leaded is easier and this is not a production line | 40 | Hackstore |
| Flux | No-clean paste or pen | 30 | Hackstore |
| Wire strippers | 10–24 AWG | 60 | Hackstore |
| Flush cutters | — | 35 | Hackstore |
| Heat-shrink assortment | 2–12 mm, plus a lighter or heat gun | 40 | Hackstore |
| Hex drivers | **1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 mm.** Every screw on a drone is one of these | 60 | KSP, 4Project |
| Precision screwdriver set | — | 59 | KSP |
| **Multimeter** | Continuity beep, DC volts, DC amps to 10 A | 120–250 | Hackstore |
| **LiPo safety bag** | Large enough for a 6S 5000 mAh (155 × 50 × 50 mm) | 50 | C-Hobby |
| **Safety glasses** | Worn every time a propeller is on | 25 | Any hardware shop |
| Thread-lock | Blue (removable), for every motor screw | 25 | Any hardware shop |
| Digital scale | 0.1 g resolution, 2 kg range. You will weigh every component in [04.02](../04-frame-and-assembly/04.02-mass-and-center-of-mass.md) | 60 | KSP |

**Cheapest path ≈ ₪600 · recommended ≈ ₪900** (station instead of a plain iron, a better
multimeter, a heat gun).

## Buy later

| Item | Buy before | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Helping hands / PCB vise | [04.03](../04-frame-and-assembly/04.03-wiring-harness.md) | Soldering a harness with two hands and no third is how you get burns |
| Fume extractor or a fan | [04.03](../04-frame-and-assembly/04.03-wiring-harness.md) | You will be soldering for hours |
| Digital calipers | [04.01](../04-frame-and-assembly/04.01-frames.md) | Measuring prop clearance and standoff lengths |
| Torque driver (0.2–2 N·m) | [04.05](../04-frame-and-assembly/04.05-building-hermon.md) | Optional. Over-torquing a motor screw into a stator is a classic first-build failure |
| Prop balancer | [02.09](../02-motors-escs-props/02.09-heat-vibration-noise.md) | ₪40 and it removes a whole class of vibration problem |

## Not a tool, but buy it with the tools

A **notebook or a build log file**. [04.05](../04-frame-and-assembly/04.05-building-hermon.md)
makes you document the build; the wiring photo you take on the day you solder it is the photo
you will want six months later when a wire chafes through.
